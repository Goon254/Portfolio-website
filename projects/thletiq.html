ThletIQ

A free training platform that builds a personalised gym program from your experience, goals, schedule and equipment, then tracks the work you do against it.

Role
Design, engineering, deployment
Stack
Next.js, React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL on Neon, Prisma, Clerk, Zustand, Recharts, Vercel
Status
Live
Link
thletiq.com

Why

A friend asked me to write him a workout plan. Rather than a spreadsheet, I built the tool I wished existed: structured programming without the subscription fee that every big fitness app charges for it. I needed the structure myself.

What it does

  • Program generation. Onboarding captures experience, goals, weekly days and available equipment. The generator maps goals to a split, selects from a database of 200+ exercises with equipment substitutions, and sets volume and progression for a 4 to 12 week block.
  • Logging. Sets, reps, weight and RPE per session, with personal records tracked automatically.
  • Analytics. Weekly volume trends and muscle-group distribution on a Recharts dashboard.
  • Substitutions. No barbell? The program swaps in a dumbbell or bodyweight alternative for the same movement pattern.

How it is built

Next.js App Router with route handlers for program generation, workout logging, progress and the exercise catalogue. Prisma models users, programs, workouts and exercises on Neon Postgres, with a self-relation for exercise substitutes. Clerk handles auth, Zustand holds client state, and the whole thing deploys on Vercel.
