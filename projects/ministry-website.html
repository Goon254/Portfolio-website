Ministry Website

A website for Heaven Targeter Ministries, a church organisation in Kenya. My first external client contract: requirements, design, build, review and handoff across time zones.

Role
Contract developer
Stack
Next.js, Vercel
Status
Delivered
Link
View the site

The brief

The client found me through East African Bites and asked for a professional site: services, mission and community information, a contact form for inquiries and prayer requests, a fast mobile experience for the congregation, and content that non-technical staff could update.

How it went

  • Week 1. Discovery call on goals, audience and identity.
  • Week 2. Written spec with wireframes for approval.
  • Weeks 3 to 4. Build: responsive Next.js site, contact forms, editable content.
  • Week 5. Staging review, feedback, revisions.
  • Week 6. Launch on Vercel, documentation, and training staff to update content.

What I learned

Technical skill was half the job. Asking why behind each request, sending written summaries after every call, showing progress early, and explaining trade-offs in plain language mattered as much as the code. Deliver what you promise, when you said you would.
