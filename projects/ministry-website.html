Project Context

🌍 First Professional Client Contract
I acquired a small contract to develop a website for a church organization in Kenya. This was my first experience working with an external client — navigating requirements gathering, managing expectations across time zones, and delivering a product that matched their vision.

The project taught me that technical skills are only half the equation. Clear communication, regular updates, and understanding the client's non-technical perspective were just as critical as the code itself.

Key Learnings

🎯
Ask Clarifying Questions
Don't assume requirements. Ask "why" behind each feature request to understand the actual need, not just the stated want.
🔄
Regular Check-ins
Weekly updates prevent misalignment. Show progress early and often — clients appreciate transparency over perfect surprises.
📝
Document Everything
Written requirements protect both parties. Email summaries after calls ensure everyone's on the same page.
🎓
Educate the Client
Explain technical constraints in plain language. Help them understand trade-offs between features, timeline, and budget.
