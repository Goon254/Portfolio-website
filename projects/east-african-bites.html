East African Bites

An ordering platform for my own cottage kitchen. Customers order chapati and mandazi, capacity is checked per delivery zone, and they get SMS updates from confirmed to ready.

Role
Owner, engineering and operations
Stack
Next.js, Supabase (PostgreSQL), Twilio, Zustand, Zod, Vercel
Status
Live
Link
east-african-bites.vercel.app

Why

After graduation, job hunting as an international student meant visa waiting periods. I bake well, people kept saying so, and most people around me were too busy to cook. So I built a way for them to order and for me to pay the bills. It became a real production system handling real orders and deliveries.

What it does

  • Ordering. Menu, cart and checkout with React Hook Form and Zod validation, persisted locally so a refresh does not lose a basket.
  • Capacity by zone. A Supabase RPC checks the daily limit for each delivery zone before an order is accepted, and zone fees are applied automatically.
  • SMS updates. Twilio messages at confirmed, preparing and ready, plus phone verification for order lookup.
  • Admin dashboard. Real-time order management and financial tracking for the kitchen side.

What I learned

Operating your own product teaches things a class cannot. The first weekend sold out faster than the capacity limit allowed, which became a hotfix. A timezone bug moved delivery slots by an hour. Every fix came from a customer, which is the fastest feedback loop there is.
