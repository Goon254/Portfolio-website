About

Briefly

I grew up in Eldama Ravine, Kenya, moved to New Hampshire in 2021, and graduated with a BS in Computer Science from Southern New Hampshire University in 2025. I ran NCAA Division II cross-country there until injuries retired me.

Most of what I know came from building things that had to work for other people: an app with users on two continents, a payments and API business, and client sites. I care about the details that make software feel finished.
