About

From Eldama Ravine to shipping software.

I am a software engineer in Manchester, New Hampshire. I like building the whole thing: the app, the backend, the payments, the deploy, and the part where real people use it.

Background

I grew up in Eldama Ravine, Kenya. I moved to New Hampshire in December 2021 to study computer science at Southern New Hampshire University and wrote my first line of code the following January. I graduated in November 2025.

Alongside school I ran NCAA Division II cross-country, fifty to sixty miles a week, until hip and knee injuries ended that. Running taught me the thing I rely on most in engineering: keep going when it is uncomfortable, and fix the problem tomorrow with a clear head rather than tonight with a tired one.

Every project on this site started as a real need. Diaspora Link exists because settling into a new country through scattered WhatsApp groups was hard. East African Bites paid bills after graduation. ThletIQ began as a workout plan for a friend. That is how I like to work: find the problem, build the fix, keep improving it once people depend on it.
