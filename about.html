About Me

From village innovation to cutting-edge robotics — where endurance meets engineering.

Growing up in Eldama Ravine, a small Kenyan village where resourcefulness wasn't optional, I learned that the best solutions come from combining persistence with creativity. Daily runs through rural landscapes taught me more than fitness; they taught me how to pace for the long haul and push through when it matters most.

As a software engineer specializing in robotics, I bring that same marathon mindset to every system I build. My work spans from community platforms like Event-Hive and Diaspora Link to the precise world of embedded systems and machine intelligence.

Whether I'm programming PID controllers for autonomous vehicle stabilization, implementing computer vision for real-time object recognition, or designing interfaces that make complex robotics feel intuitive, I'm driven by one goal: creating technology that doesn't just compute — it responds, adapts, and enhances human experience.

What sets me apart isn't just versatility across the full stack — from embedded C++ to modern web frameworks — it's my builder's philosophy: treating every challenge like a marathon, balancing calculated persistence with creative problem-solving.

Every project I tackle, from village innovation to cutting-edge robotics, reinforces my belief that the most powerful technology emerges when endurance meets engineering — always crossing the finish line with solutions that work in the real world.
